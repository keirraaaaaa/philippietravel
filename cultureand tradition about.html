Culture and Traditions

The Philippines has a rich and colorful culture influenced by Malay, Spanish, Chinese, American, and other Asian traditions.
Festivals: Filipinos celebrate festivals such as Sinulog, Ati-Atihan, and Pahiyas.
Traditional clothing: Barong Tagalog is a traditional formal outfit for men, while the Baro't Saya is a traditional outfit for women.
Food: Popular Filipino dishes include adobo, sinigang, lechon, lumpia, and pancit.
Family values: Filipinos value family relationships, respect for elders, and togetherness.
Religion: Christianity, particularly Roman Catholicism, is widely practiced, alongside Islam and other religions.
Traditions: Celebrations such as Christmas, fiestas, weddings, and family reunions are important parts of Filipino life.
