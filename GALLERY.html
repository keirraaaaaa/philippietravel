Where Every Island tells a story

Come explore the Philippines

Tourists should explore the Philippines for its unmatched tropical beauty, rich culture, and welcoming people. With over 7,000 islands, the country offers world-famous white-sand beaches, crystal-clear lagoons in Palawan and Boracay, and some of the richest marine biodiversity on Earth for diving and snorkeling.

Come visit us at...


BORACAY


Name: Boracay Island
Location: Malay, Aklan, Western Visayas, Philippines.
Informative Description:
Boracay is a world-famous tropical island known for its powdery white sand, crystal-clear blue waters, and beautiful beaches. It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in the Philippines, offering relaxation, adventure, and exciting nightlife.
Attractions:
White Beach
Puka Shell Beach
Willy's Rock
D'Mall
Mount Luho
Ariel's Point
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
To enjoy its beautiful white-sand beaches.
To swim and experience clear blue waters.
To participate in water activities such as parasailing, snorkeling, and island hopping.
To enjoy breathtaking sunsets.
To experience restaurants, shopping, and nightlife.


PUERTO PRINCESSA


Name: Puerto Princesa
Location: Palawan, MIMAROPA Region, Philippines.
Informative Description:

Puerto Princesa is the capital city of Palawan, known for its natural beauty, clean environment, and rich biodiversity. It is famous for the Puerto Princesa Underground River, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature.

Attractions:


Puerto Princesa Underground River
Honda Bay
Baker's Hill
Palawan Wildlife Rescue and Conservation Center
Iwahig Firefly Watching
Mitra's Ranch
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
To explore the famous Underground River.
To experience island hopping and snorkeling.
To discover beautiful beaches and natural landscapes.
To see wildlife and marine animals.


To enjoy nature, adventure, and eco-tourism activities.


100 ISLANDS


Name: Hundred Islands National Park
Location: Alaminos City, Pangasinan, Ilocos Region, Philippines.
Informative Description:
Hundred Islands National Park is a famous tourist destination consisting of 124 islands and islets during low tide and 123 during high tide. It is known for its beautiful limestone islands, clear waters, white-sand beaches, and rich marine life. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.
Attractions:
Governor's Island
Quezon Island
Children's Island
Marcos Island
Lopez Island
Pilgrimage Island
Cave exploration and snorkeling areas
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
To experience island hopping and explore different islands.
To enjoy swimming and snorkeling.
To see beautiful rock formations and natural scenery.
To experience kayaking, ziplining, and other water activities.
To relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.


CHOCOLATE HILLS


Name: Chocolate Hills
Location: Carmen, Bohol, Central Visayas, Philippines.
Informative Description:
The Chocolate Hills are one of the most famous natural wonders in the Philippines. They consist of more than 1,200 cone-shaped hills spread across the landscape of Bohol. During the rainy season, the hills are covered with green grass, but during the dry season, they turn brown, making them look like giant chocolate mounds. This is how they got their name.

Attractions:


Chocolate Hills Viewpoint
Chocolate Hills Complex
Carmen View Deck
ATV and buggy rides
Scenic countryside views
Nearby Tarsier Conservation Areas
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
To witness one of the Philippines' most unique natural wonders.
To enjoy breathtaking panoramic views.
To take pictures of the unusual chocolate-like hills.
To experience ATV rides and outdoor adventures.
To learn about the geological history and natural beauty of Bohol.
